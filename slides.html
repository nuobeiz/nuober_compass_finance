The Problem

"Can I afford this?" is the wrong question.

🏦

Banks only check if you qualify

Lender approval doesn't mean the purchase is strategically sound for your full financial picture.

📊

Spreadsheets are static

No real-time feedback, no rubric system, no live connection between your profile and each decision.

🤔

Rent vs. Buy intuition fails

Most people compare monthly cost only — ignoring the opportunity cost of the down payment sitting outside equity markets.

What was missing
1
A single tool that cross-references income, savings, debt, and credit score against any purchase in real time.
2
Rules grounded in actual underwriting standards — not generic "afford 3× income" rules of thumb.
3
A fair Rent vs. Buy model that prices the true opportunity cost of locking capital in a down payment.
4
Persistent state so a financial analysis can be built incrementally — not re-entered from scratch every session.
The Solution

Four modules. One financial profile.

All modules share a global FinancialProfile — a change in income or savings propagates to every rubric instantly.

Data Sources

Historical data & regulatory standards

📈 Equity Market Returns

S&P 500 nominal avg (1928–2023) ~10%/yr
Real avg ≈ 6.8%/yr · Source: Shiller, JP Morgan, Vanguard
App default: 10% nominal (adjustable 1–20%)

🏘️ Home Price Appreciation

Case-Shiller national avg (1987–2023) ~4.3%/yr
Real avg ≈ 1.5%/yr · Source: S&P CoreLogic, FHFA HPI, FRED
App default: 4% nominal (adjustable 0–10%)

🏠 Property Carrying Cost Rates

Property Tax 1.1% of value/yr (ATTOM 2023) Homeowner's Insurance 0.5% of value/yr (NAIC 2022) PMI (if <20% down) 0.7% of loan/yr (Urban Institute) Closing Costs 3% of price (CFPB midpoint) Annual Maintenance 1% of price/yr ("1% Rule")

📋 Regulatory Standards Used

28% Front-End DTI Fannie Mae / Freddie Mac Selling Guide
36% Back-End DTI (conv.) Fannie Mae standard; FHA allows 43%
43% QM hard cap CFPB 12 CFR Part 1026
VA Residual Income Table 38 CFR § 36.4340, Table 41A
VA Funding Fee 2.15% 38 CFR § 36.4312 (first use, 0% down)
3–6 month reserve guideline CFPB, HUD, Fannie Mae B3-4.3-04
Price-to-Income ≤ 3–4× Harvard JCHS; CFP Board guidance

🧮 Post-Tax Income Approximation

22% effective rate based on IRS Statistics of Income data for $50k–$150k earners.

Net Monthly = (Annual Income / 12) × 0.78

Rubric Contract — The Engine Core
{ id: 'front_end_dti', compute: (profile, purchase) => ({ value: (piti / income) * 100, display: '23.4%', benchmark: '≤ 28%' }), evaluate: (result) => result.value <= 28 ? 'pass' : result.value <= 32 ? 'stretch' : 'fail', advice: { pass: 'Within the 28% guideline.', stretch: 'Slightly elevated — monitor.', fail: 'Exceeds safe limits.' } }

Why config over hard-code?

Adding an Auto Loan or Business Equipment module requires only a new array of rule objects. Zero UI changes — RubricCard and ProFormaSummary are fully generic consumers.
